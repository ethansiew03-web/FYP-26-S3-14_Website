Additional Features

View your history anywhere

Scan results are saved to your account, not your PC, so your history follows you to any machine you sign in on.

Manage your team

Bring your whole team under one organisation. Admins can invite members to join, and every member's scan results are saved to the shared account, so the admin has a full view of the team's testing in one place.

Export your scan reports

Turn any scan into a clean HTML or PDF report. Findings, severity levels and suggested fixes are laid out clearly, so the report is ready to share with your team, attach to a ticket, or include in project documentation.

Spot the difference

Put two scans of the same app side by side to see what changed. You can quickly check which issues were fixed, which are still there, and whether any new ones appeared after an update.

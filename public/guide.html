User Guide

How to sign up, get BioAudit, sign in, and add your team.

1. Sign up

Student account (Free)

  1. Click Sign Up at the top of the page and choose Student.
  2. Enter your name, choose your university, and enter your university email address.
  3. Choose a password of at least 12 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit and a symbol, then type it again to confirm.
  4. Press Send code. We email a six-digit code to your university address. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then press Resend code.
  5. Type the code into the box (pasting it works), tick the Terms and Conditions box, and press Create account.

Student access lasts 12 months. Your university email needs to be verified again every year.

Organisation account (Paid)

  1. Click Sign Up and choose Organisation. Only the person who will manage the team and pay for it (the admin) should do this.
  2. Enter your name, email, a password (same rules as above) and your organisation's name, then tick the Terms and Conditions box.
  3. Press Create account. You are taken to Stripe to pay for the yearly plan.
  4. After payment you return to your dashboard. The plan switches on within a short moment of Stripe confirming the payment. If checkout did not open, use Complete payment on the dashboard.

2. Download BioAudit

  1. Log in on this website.
  2. On the Overview tab of your dashboard, press Download for Windows. This always fetches the latest version.
  3. When the download finishes, double-click BioAudit.exe. There is nothing to install.

If Windows shows a warning, choose More info, then Run anyway. BioAudit is a student project and is not yet code signed.

3. Sign in on BioAudit

  1. Open BioAudit. A welcome screen appears with three tabs: Create account, Join with an invite and Sign in.
  2. Choose Sign in and enter the same email and password you used on this website.
  3. Optional: tick Keep me signed in on this computer to skip this next time. Leave it off on a shared or public computer.
  4. Forgotten your password? Press Forgot password?, then Send code, and enter the six-digit code we email you together with a new password.

BioAudit saves every scan to your account, so you need to be signed in before it will scan anything.

4. Add members to your organisation

Only the organisation's admin can invite people, and the organisation's subscription must be paid first.

As the admin

  1. Sign in to BioAudit and open the Team tab.
  2. Go to Invitations and enter the new member's email address.
  3. BioAudit creates a one-time invitation token. Send it to that person.

As the new member

  1. Open BioAudit and choose the Join with an invite tab.
  2. Enter the email the invitation was sent to, choose a password, and paste the invitation token.
  3. Press the join button. You are now part of the organisation and covered by its subscription.

Admins can also promote, suspend or remove members from the Members sub-tab, and cancel or renew the subscription from Subscription.

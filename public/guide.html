Set up your account

How to set up your BioAudit account

Set up your account

1. Sign up 2. Download BioAudit 3. Sign in on BioAudit 4. Add members

Set up your phone

1. USB debugging 2. Get ADB 3. Phone not detected

1. Sign up

Student account (Free)

  1. Click Sign Up at the top of the page and choose Student.
  2. Enter your name, choose your university, and enter your university email address.
  3. Choose a password of at least 12 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a digit and a symbol, then type it again to confirm.
  4. Click Send code. A six-digit code will be emailed to your university address. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, or click Resend code.
  5. Type or paste the code into the box, check the Terms and Conditions box, and click Create account.

Student access lasts 3 years from the sign up date. After that, your account will be suspended in the app, but access to dashboard will still be available.

Organisation account (Paid)

  1. Click Sign Up and choose Organisation.
  2. Enter your name, email, a password and your organisation's name. Only the details of the person managing the team (the admin) should be entered here.
  3. Check the Terms and Conditions box.
  4. Click Create account. You will be taken to Stripe to complete your purchase.
  5. After payment, you will be returned to your dashboard. The plan switches on within a short moment of Stripe confirming the payment. If checkout did not open, use Complete payment on the dashboard.

2. Download BioAudit

There is no need to install anything for BioAudit as it is a single .exe file.

  1. Log in on this website.
  2. On the Overview tab of your dashboard, click Download for Windows.
  3. When download is complete, double click BioAudit.exe to open the app.
  4. Windows Defender SmartScreen will open. Click on More infor, then Run anyway.

Only when BioAudit is opened for the first time, Windows Defender SmartScreen will appear. BioAudit is a student project and is not yet code signed.

3. Sign in on BioAudit

  1. Open BioAudit. A welcome screen appears with three tabs: Create account, Join with an invite and Sign in.
  2. Choose Sign in and enter the same email and password you used on this website.
  3. Optional: check Keep me signed in on this computer to skip this next time. Leave it off on a shared or public computer.
  4. If you lose your password, click Forgot password?, then Send code. Enter the six-digit code sent to your email and enter your new password.

BioAudit saves every scan to your account and you can access all your scans on the website's dashboard as well.

4. Add members to your organisation

Only the organisation's admin can invite people, and the organisation's subscription must be paid first.

As the admin

  1. Sign in to BioAudit and open the Team tab.
  2. Go to Invitations and enter the new member's email address.
  3. A one-time invitation token will be sent to the member's email address.

As the new member

  1. Open BioAudit and choose the Join with an invite tab.
  2. Enter the email the invitation was sent to, choose a password, and paste the invitation token.
  3. Click the join button.

Admins can also promote, suspend or remove members from the Members sub-tab, and cancel or renew the subscription from Subscription.

Set up your phone

This is only needed assess an app on a real phone (for Access a device feature). Only available on Android mobile devices.

1. Turn on USB debugging

  1. On your phone, open Settings, then About phone, and tap Build number seven times until it says you are now a developer. On Samsung phones, Build number is under Software information.
  2. Go back to Settings, open Developer options (usually under System) and turn on USB debugging.
  3. Connect the phone to your computer with a USB cable that transfers data. A charge only cable will not work.
  4. The first time BioAudit looks for your phone, the phone asks Allow USB debugging?. Unlock it and tap Allow.

2. Get ADB

ADB is Google's tool that lets BioAudit talk to your phone. It is only needed for assessing a phone.

The easy way

  1. In BioAudit, open Assess a device.
  2. If ADB is not set up on your computer, a Download ADB button appears next to the device list. Click it and confirm. BioAudit downloads Google's Platform-Tools (about 7 MB) into its own folder. Nothing is installed on Windows.
  3. When BioAudit says ADB is ready, click Refresh. Your phone should appear in the list.

Manual set up

  1. Download SDK Platform-Tools for Windows from developer.android.com and unzip it, for example to C:\platform-tools.
  2. Click the Start button, type Edit the system environment variables, open it and click Environment Variables.
  3. Under User variables, select Path, click Edit, then New, and paste the folder path, for example C:\platform-tools. Click OK on every window.
  4. Close and reopen BioAudit. To check, open Command Prompt and type adb version. If it shows a version number, ADB is ready.

3. If your phone is not detected

Click Refresh after each fix. The device list tells you what is wrong:

  • "phone found, not allowed yet": unlock the phone and tap Allow on its USB debugging prompt.
  • "phone found but offline": unplug the phone and plug it back in.
  • "no device — check USB debugging": make sure USB debugging is on, use a data cable and try another USB port. Windows also needs your phone's USB driver, which it normally installs when you connect the phone.
  • "ADB is not set up on this computer": click Download ADB. If you set ADB up yourself, open Command Prompt and type adb devices. If Windows says adb is not recognized, the Path step did not work, so repeat it or use Download ADB instead.

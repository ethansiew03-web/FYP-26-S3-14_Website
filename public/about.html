Our Story

Biometric authentication security is one of the most important features when it comes to mobile app security, yet few tools focus specifically on checking whether that security is implemented properly.

BioAudit probes Android apps the way a real attacker would, without needing source code or root access. It uncovers the side-channel leaks, response inconsistencies, and authentication bypasses that let attackers slip past a "secure-looking" biometric gate, the kind of flaws that do not show up unless you go looking for them the way an attacker actually would.

Every finding is backed by deterministic, rule-based detection and then explained in plain language, so teams do not just get a list of problems, they get a clear path to fixing them.

With BioAudit, your app will truly be secure.
